# Practical 13 Summary

**Introduction:**

This practical compares the similarity between human DLX5, mouse DLX5, and a random protein sequence using the BLOSUM62 scoring matrix.

All three sequences are 289 amino acids long, matching the length of the human DLX5 protein. Human DLX5 localises to the nucleus, consistent with its role as a homeobox transcription factor.

**Key Result:**

1.  **Human DLX5 vs Mouse DLX5**
    - Alignment score: 1490
    - Percentage of identical amino acids: 96.54%
2.  **Human DLX5 vs Random Sequence**
    - Alignment score: -158
    - Percentage of identical amino acids: 9.34%
3.  **Mouse DLX5 vs Random Sequence**
    - Alignment score: -165
    - Percentage of identical amino acids: 9.00%

**Key finding:**

1. Obviously, the human and mouse sequences are very similar, they have a blosum62 score for 1490, and the identity for 96.54%, indicating a strong evolutionary relationship between the two species. 

2. In contrast, the scores and identities for comparisons with the random sequence are low, suggesting no meaningful similarity.

**Conclusion:**
> human DLX5 and mouse DLX5 have close relationship. Their high sequence similarity reflects the evolutionary conservation of this homeobox transcription factor across mammals.
